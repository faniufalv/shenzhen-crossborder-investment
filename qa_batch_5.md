## Q1: [Securities Crime] How to apply for disability assessment after a traffic accident?

**A**: Traffic accident disability assessment process: 1) Apply to qualified judicial appraisal institution after treatment completion; 2) Submit accident report, medical records, imaging materials; 3) Institution issues assessment within 30 working days; 4) Dissatisfied parties can request re-assessment.

**法律依据**: 《道路交通安全法》

**领域**: 交通事故 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Traffic Accidents] For Expatriates: What legal considerations should overseas Chinese have when buying property in China?

**A**: Overseas Chinese purchasing property in China should note: 1) Confirm purchase eligibility, some cities have restrictions; 2) Overseas income certificates need notarization and authentication; 3) Fund remittance requires foreign exchange registration; 4) Property registration should use Chinese name plus passport number.

**法律依据**: 《城市房地产管理法》

**领域**: 房产纠纷 | **语言**: English
